Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking healthcare with MedicalCare

How can I book a doctor appointment on MedicalCare?

Search for a doctor by specialty, city or name. Choose an available time slot and complete your booking.

Is booking appointments on MedicalCare free?

Booking through the platform is free. You only pay the doctor's consultation fee or other selected healthcare services.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes. You can manage your appointment from your account depending on the doctor and appointment policy.

How are doctors verified?

Doctor applications are reviewed using their submitted medical license and identification documents before approval.
