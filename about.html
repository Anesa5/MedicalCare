About MedicalCare

Pakistan's leading digital healthcare platform

Our Mission

MedicalCare was founded with a simple mission: to make quality healthcare accessible to every person in Pakistan. We connect millions of patients with verified doctors, hospitals, and diagnostic labs across the country — all from the comfort of their homes.

Our Story

Since our launch, MedicalCare has grown to become Pakistan's largest healthcare platform. We started with a handful of doctors in Lahore and today we serve patients in over 30 cities with more than 25,000 verified doctors and 500+ hospitals on board.

Our platform has facilitated over 50 million patient searches and millions of appointments, making healthcare simpler, faster, and more transparent for everyone.
